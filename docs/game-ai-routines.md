# What the game's own computer player still decides for the mod's players

Made 2026-11-10 to decide what to switch off (the player, 2026-11-09: "should we just disable the ingame ai fully when our is
active?"). Read from the decompilation of the 2017 update, spec/, the mod's hooks and the shipped defaults: no game was run for
it and Art of Conquest's routines were not compared. "The mod's players" are those with `is_vanilla(pl)` false.

- **REPLACED**: the game's decision doesn't run for them or its result is overwritten whole.
- **FILTERED**: the game still originates the decision; the mod vets, reorders, adds to or changes its inputs or outputs.
- **ALONE**: no hook on it and nothing of the mod feeds it. **ALONE (gate)**: the decision is the game's and unvetted, only
  `hook_place` or `hook_train_units`, which everything passes, can refuse it afterwards.

76 rows: 9 REPLACED, 37 FILTERED (4 of them ALONE (gate) below), 29 ALONE, 1 unknown. A count of rows, not of importance.

## War

- `ar_on(pl)` (armies.c:269) = not vanilla, `mod_armies` (default on), `navy_land` (navy.c:282: map kind known and not NV_ISLANDS, navy_detect fixes it once at 10 s or later, up to 180 s while an enemy has no buildings). "Islands" = NV_ISLANDS (every enemy across water): ar_on false, the commander keeps the land soldiers.
- Land soldier = mass.c ms_land_soldier: is_soldier and not airplane, helicopter, naval, submarine, naval transport, carrier, naval citizen. Siege counts when is_soldier says so.
- The hold (armies.c armies_tick, every 2 s): `player_hold_unit` sets ai_held (+0x341) and takes the soldier out of the commander's pool (unregister_unit 0x471F8E). `hook_unit_to_group` refuses offers of those soldiers. This is one effect on the whole combat manager. ALONE below means nothing in the mod touches that routine, its arguments or its result; on ar_on players it may simply find no land soldiers in its groups (noted where that matters).
- The intelligence files (EECPIntelligencePlayerFile, update 0x47CBCC every 30 s or forced; docs/engine-notes.md "The intelligence files see everything"): the game fills them from the other player's whole unit list. They feed the target pick of 0x46F4FF (rows 2-3), the unit selector's counter vote 0x49352A (what attack and defence groups request: row 26's lists, which army.c FILL trains from) and the groups' strength comparisons. FILTERED by `honest_intel` (intel.c, default on since 2026-11-11, needs honest_vision): for the mod's players a file on an enemy holds only the units the AI sees or remembers; its known area is seeded by the first unit counted. Left: a remembered unit's place is where it is now.
- `hook_cp_update` (engine/scenario_ai.c) clears the scenario flag during the whole update for mod players (`scenario_ai` default on). It only matters in scenarios (0x431141 and other readers); not counted as influence below.
- Shipped defaults relied on: mod_armies, army_stage, attack_commit, avoid_towers, attack_weak_spots, attack_leash, attack_breach, attack_ignore_air, attack_siege, danger_routes, avoid_lost_fights, fight_memory, honest_vision, outdated_attack, landing, navy (navy_fleet=big), muster, counter_siege, counter_fire, citizen_militia, ai_diplomacy, answer_war, surrender, resign_spoils, fishing_boat: on. fall_back = hospital, tower_cover, fleet_cover. citizen_attacks, wave_focus, front_army, attack_retarget, hold_town: off. army_mass empty, expand_islands empty, towns = resource_towns, spread. AS_ON 1 (air force), SK_ON 0 (skirmish). attack_interval 20-25, first_attack left (0), attack_on_contact 0, attack_for_resources 0, attack_revenge -0.01 (= leave the game's value).
