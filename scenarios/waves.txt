# Stress waves: one AI (P2) builds up for 60 minutes in peace (ai_ignore: it leaves the idle P1 alone), then P1
# gets ever stronger copies of P2's own army near P1's base (wave, % of P2's soldiers by type) and they charge
# P2's town (charge: every 10 s, see attack.c STRESS WAVES). The minute P2
# falls (game over) is the score; count 2 after each wave shows what is left.
